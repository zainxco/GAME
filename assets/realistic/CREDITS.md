# Realistic courtyard assets

Downloaded from Poly Haven for the LASA single-level playtest. Assets are CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Asphalt 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02
- Brick Wall 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/brick_wall_02
- Concrete Wall 006: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_006
- Abandoned Slipway HDRI: https://polyhaven.com/a/abandoned_slipway
- Barrel 03: https://polyhaven.com/a/barrel_03
- Concrete Road Barrier: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_road_barrier
- Wooden Crate 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_crate_01

All assets are served locally. 1K color, normal and roughness maps are used for the surfaces. The HDRI supplies the sky and environment lighting. Architecture and small details were built for this game. Existing gun and character licenses remain as documented in the project README. RGBELoader is from Three.js 0.160.0, covered by THREE-LICENSE.txt.
